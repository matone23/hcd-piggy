📅 Routine Mode
Daily check-in competition over multiple days. Upload proof photo each day to earn points based on your intensity level (Min/More/Max).
Best for: Building daily habits like exercise, reading, meditation
Example: "14-day morning workout"
How it works: Upload daily proof photo → Earn points (2/5/10 based on intensity) → Highest total points when time ends wins
🎯 1-Day Challenge
Complete a to-do list in a single day. Create tasks with expected time, check them off as you go, and compete on completion rate.
Best for: Sprint tasks, weekend projects, to-do lists
Example: "Saturday deep clean - 5 tasks (180 min total)"
How it works: Add tasks with time estimates → Check off & upload proof as you complete → Highest completion rate by end of day wins
